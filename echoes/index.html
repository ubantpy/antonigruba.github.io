Just speak.
Echoes does the rest.

Open the app, tap record, and talk. Echoes transcribes your voice using Whisper, so you don't have to type and nothing to format. A daily entry takes under two minutes, and the words are yours - stored privately on your device.

Understand

Mood analysis,
without the guesswork.

Every entry is analysed by a sentiment model that maps your words to one of five emotional states - from great to very low. Each day gets a coloured marker. Over weeks, the colours tell a story you might not have noticed on your own.

echoes of your life.

At the end of each week and month, Echoes generates a written reflection of your entries - what themes surfaced, how your mood shifted, and what you returned to. Not a summary of facts, but a mirror held up to your own words.

The developer

Antoni Gruba

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of London building Echoes as my final-year AI project. I wanted a journaling app that didn't make me stare at a blank text box - so I built one you can just talk to. It runs on your device, keeps your words private, and gets smarter the longer you use it.
