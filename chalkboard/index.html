Discover climbing gyms wherever you are - near home, or in a city you've never been to. Search the map, read what climbers say, save the ones worth returning to.

Get it on Google Play
Rankings

THE BEST
GYMS NEARBY

Gyms are ranked by a score that factors in both their rating and how many people reviewed them - so a 4.9 from twelve visitors ranks lower than a 4.7 from three hundred. Adjust the search radius from 1 to 25 km and the list updates instantly.

Profiles

EVERYTHING
BEFORE YOU GO

Each gym profile shows opening hours, address, ratings, user reviews, and a direct link to Google Maps for directions. If the gym has a website, that's there too. No searching around - it's all in one place.

Save and track

Mark any gym as a favourite, visited, or somewhere you want to go. Your lists are stored on your device - nothing synced, nothing shared.

No account needed

Everything lives on your phone. No sign-up, no email, no password. Open it, find a gym, go climb.
