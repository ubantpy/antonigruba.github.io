Echoes: Voice Journal & Mood

A voice journal you just talk to. Speak about your day for a minute and Echoes transcribes it, gives the day one of five mood characters, and writes short weekly and monthly summaries. Built with React Native (Expo) and TypeScript, with serverless AI functions for transcription, sentiment analysis and summaries.
