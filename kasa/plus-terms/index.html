Kasa Budget Plus Terms

Published: 22 November 2026 · Kasa Budget Plus is not on sale yet. These terms apply from the day it goes on sale on Google Play.

The short version

  • Kasa Budget is free to use. Kasa Budget Plus is an optional upgrade, bought through Google Play.
  • Subscriptions renew until you cancel them in Google Play. The 7-day free trial is for your first yearly or monthly subscription only.
  • Lifetime means one payment, for as long as Kasa Budget is offered on Google Play.
  • Nothing you add is ever deleted. If Plus ends, you still see everything and can export it.

Who sells Kasa Budget Plus

Kasa Budget and Kasa Budget Plus are made by Antoni Gruba, an independent developer. Plus is sold through Google Play, which takes the payment, handles taxes and sends your receipt. Google Play's own terms apply to the purchase as well as these terms. For questions, contact antonigruba.dev@gmail.com.

What's free and what Plus adds

The free version has one wallet and one budget, unlimited transactions, categories and labels, scheduled payments and reminders, a week-by-week or month-by-month overview, search, manual backups, export to CSV or Excel, and the passcode and privacy features.

Plus adds unlimited wallets and budgets with budget history, every chart and insight over any period, entries in other currencies, deposits and returned purchases, receipt photos and places, and automatic backups. The full, current list is on the Plus screen in the app. Plus also includes the Plus features added later.

Prices and ways to pay

  • Monthly: a subscription paid every month.
  • Yearly: a subscription paid every year.
  • Lifetime: one payment, no subscription.

The price in your currency, including any tax, is shown in the app and by Google Play before you pay. Prices may change. If a subscription price goes up, Google Play tells you beforehand and, where the law or Google requires it, asks you to accept the new price; otherwise you can cancel before it applies.

Free trial

If you haven't subscribed to Kasa Budget Plus before, a yearly or monthly subscription can start with 7 days free. Google Play decides who can get the trial, and it is offered once per Google account. If you cancel in Google Play before the 7 days end, you aren't charged. Otherwise the subscription starts and is charged when the trial ends.

Subscriptions: renewing and cancelling

A subscription renews by itself at the end of each month or year, and Google Play charges the payment method on your Google account, until you cancel it. You can cancel or change your plan at any time in Google Play, or from "Manage subscription" on the Plus screen in the app. When you cancel, Plus stays on until the end of the period you have already paid for. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

Lifetime purchase

Lifetime is a single payment. It gives you Kasa Budget Plus, including Plus features added later, on any phone signed in to the same Google account, for as long as Kasa Budget is offered on Google Play and the developer supports it. It is a licence to use Plus, not a promise that the app will be made forever.

A day of Plus from an ad

Without buying, you can watch one video ad to the end to get Kasa Budget Plus for 24 hours. When the day ends you can watch another. Days don't add up, and an ad closed early gives no Plus. Whether an ad is available is up to Google, so this may not always work. How ads use your information is explained in the privacy policy.

Your purchase on a new phone

Plus belongs to the Google account you bought it with, not to the phone. On a new phone, sign in to Google Play with the same account, install Kasa Budget and tap "Restore purchases" on the Plus screen. Your entries are moved separately, with a backup file. Kasa Budget has no account of its own.

If Plus ends

Nothing you added is deleted. You still see all your wallets and entries, and the totals and overview include them. New entries go to one free wallet (the one you use most, which you can change), extra budgets pause, and features that need Plus are locked until you have Plus again. Backups and exports always include everything. When Plus comes back, everything opens again as it was.

Kasa Budget only turns Plus off when Google Play clearly reports that your account no longer owns it. Being offline, or Google Play not answering, never turns Plus off.

Refunds

Purchases are refunded under Google Play's refund policy. You can ask Google Play for a refund from your order history, and shortly after a purchase this is usually quick. If Google Play can't help, write to the email address above. Nothing in these terms limits the rights you have under the consumer law of the country you live in.

Changes

These terms may change, for example when prices or Plus features change. Changes will be published on this page with a new date. A change never takes away a lifetime purchase you have already made, or a subscription period you have already paid for.
