Kasa Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

The short version

  • Everything you enter in Kasa is stored on your phone. There is no account and no sign-up, and Kasa never connects to your bank.
  • The only thing Kasa downloads is exchange rates. That request contains nothing about you or your money.
  • Kasa has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and your information is never sold or shared.

Who is responsible

Kasa is developed by Antoni Gruba, an independent developer. If you have any questions about this policy or your information, contact antonigruba.dev@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Everything you create in Kasa is stored locally on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • Your wallets, transactions, transfers, categories, labels, notes, budgets and scheduled payments.
  • Receipt photos you attach to a transaction. Kasa keeps its own copy of each photo in its private storage.
  • The place you add to a transaction, if you choose to add one: its map position and place name.
  • Your settings, such as your main currency, reminder time and theme, and the latest exchange rates, so the app works offline.
  • Your passcode, if you set one. It is not stored as you typed it: Kasa keeps a scrambled (hashed) version in your phone's secure storage.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. It stays on your device unless you choose to export or back it up yourself, as described below. Uninstalling the app deletes everything Kasa has stored on that device. Your phone's own backup service (for example Google's device backup) may include the app's data, depending on your phone's settings.

Internet use: exchange rates

To convert between currencies, Kasa downloads the latest exchange rates from free public services: open.er-api.com and, if it is unavailable, the currency-api service, which is served through jsDelivr and Cloudflare. This happens when you open the app (at most every few hours) and when you tap to update rates in the settings.

These requests ask only for the current list of rates. They contain no information about you, your transactions or your balances. Like any website you visit, these services can see your device's IP address and the time of the request, under their own privacy policies.

Kasa makes no other internet requests of its own. It does not connect to banks, and it has no server or account system.

Location

Kasa only uses your location when you tap the location button while adding or editing a transaction. It then reads your current position once and saves it with that transaction. To show a place name instead of coordinates, Kasa asks your phone's built-in location service to look up the address. On Android this service is provided by your phone's maker or Google, and it may send the position online to do so. Kasa does not track your location in the background.

If you tap a saved place to open it on a map, its position is passed to the maps app you choose.

Permissions

Kasa asks for each permission only when you first use the feature that needs it, never when the app starts.

  • Camera: only to take a receipt photo when you choose to. If you pick a photo from your gallery instead, you do it in your phone's own photo picker, and Kasa only receives the photo you pick.
  • Location: only to add a place to a transaction, as described above.
  • Notifications and alarms: only if you turn on the reminders or alerts described below.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock: only if you turn it on, to unlock Kasa instead of typing your passcode. Your fingerprint or face is checked by your phone. Kasa never receives it, only whether the check succeeded.

If you turn on "shake to hide amounts", Kasa reads the phone's motion sensor while the app is open to notice a shake. These readings are not stored.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Kasa can send you:

  • A daily reminder to add your spending, at the time you choose.
  • A reminder on the morning a scheduled payment is due. It shows the payment's note or category and its amount.
  • An alert when a budget reaches 75% or 90%, or is exceeded. It shows the budget's name and the amount left or over.

These notifications are created on your phone by the app itself. No information is sent to the developer or to any notification service to deliver them. Because they can show amounts, they may be visible on your lock screen, depending on your phone's settings. You can turn each kind off in the app, or turn off notifications for Kasa in your device settings.

Import, export and backups

You can import transactions from a file, such as a Spendee export or a CSV or Excel file, and export your transactions to CSV or Excel. You can also save a backup file of all your data and restore it later. Files are read and created on your phone. Kasa does not upload them anywhere.

When you export or back up, your phone's share menu lets you choose where the file goes, for example a folder, a cloud drive or an email. That file then leaves Kasa's control and is handled by the service you chose. Backup and export files are not encrypted and contain your financial records, so keep them somewhere safe.

Data sharing

Your information is not shared with anyone. The developer does not collect it, does not sell, rent or trade it, does not show ads and does not track you across other apps or websites. Kasa contains no analytics or advertising tools.

Security

Kasa keeps your data in its private storage, which other apps cannot read. You can also lock Kasa with a passcode or your fingerprint, and it locks itself again shortly after you leave it. Because your data is stored on your phone, keeping your device secure, for example with a screen lock, also protects your finances.

Your choices and rights

Because your data is stored on your device, you are in control of it: you can view, change and delete your transactions and other records in the app at any time, delete all your data from the app's settings, or delete everything by uninstalling it. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights under data protection law, such as the right to ask what information is held about you or to complain to a data protection authority. The developer holds no information about you, but you are welcome to ask a question or make a request at the email address above.

Not financial advice

Kasa is a tool for keeping track of your own money. Figures such as exchange rates, projections and suggested daily amounts are estimates and can be wrong. They are not financial advice.

Children

Kasa is not intended for children under 16.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Kasa changes, for example when new features are added. Any changes will be published on this page with a new effective date.
