Kasa Budget Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · Last updated: 22 November 2026 (added Kasa Budget Plus, bought through Google Play, and the optional ad for a free day of Plus; also deposit reminders and automatic backups)

The short version

  • Everything you enter in Kasa Budget is stored on your phone. There is no account and no sign-up, and Kasa Budget never connects to your bank.
  • The only thing Kasa Budget itself downloads is exchange rates. That request contains nothing about you or your money.
  • If you buy Kasa Budget Plus, Google Play handles the payment. If you choose to watch an ad for a free day of Plus, Google shows it. Nothing about your money is ever shared with Google or anyone else.
  • Kasa Budget has no analytics and no tracking, shows no other ads, and your information is never sold.

Who is responsible

Kasa Budget is developed by Antoni Gruba, an independent developer. If you have any questions about this policy or your information, contact antonigruba.dev@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Everything you create in Kasa Budget is stored locally on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • Your wallets, transactions, transfers, categories, labels, notes, budgets and scheduled payments.
  • Receipt photos you attach to a transaction. Kasa Budget keeps its own copy of each photo in its private storage.
  • The place you add to a transaction, if you choose to add one: its map position and place name.
  • Your settings, such as your main currency, reminder time and theme, and the latest exchange rates, so the app works offline.
  • Whether you have Kasa Budget Plus, which wallet you use without it, and when a free day of Plus from an ad ends. This is kept only on the phone and is not part of backups.
  • Your passcode, if you set one. It is not stored as you typed it: Kasa Budget keeps a scrambled (hashed) version in your phone's secure storage.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. It stays on your device unless you choose to export or back it up yourself, as described below. Uninstalling the app deletes everything Kasa Budget has stored on that device. Your phone's own backup service (for example Google's device backup) may include the app's data, depending on your phone's settings.

Internet use: exchange rates

To convert between currencies, Kasa Budget downloads the latest exchange rates from free public services: open.er-api.com and, if it is unavailable, the currency-api service, which is served through jsDelivr and Cloudflare. This happens when you open the app (at most every few hours) and when you tap to update rates in the settings.

These requests ask only for the current list of rates. They contain no information about you, your transactions or your balances. Like any website you visit, these services can see your device's IP address and the time of the request, under their own privacy policies.

Apart from these, Kasa Budget only goes online through Google Play when you buy or restore Kasa Budget Plus, and through Google's ads service when you choose to watch an ad, both described below. It does not connect to banks, and it has no server or account system.

Kasa Budget Plus and Google Play

Kasa Budget is free to use. Kasa Budget Plus, an optional upgrade, is bought through Google Play, as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time purchase. Google handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy; the developer never sees your card or payment details.

To know whether you have Plus, Kasa Budget asks the Google Play app on your phone which Kasa Budget purchases your Google account owns. It does this when you open the app and when you tap "Restore purchases". As with any app sold on Google Play, the developer sees the orders in the Google Play Console. No information about your money is sent. See the Kasa Budget Plus terms for how Plus works.

Ads: only if you choose to watch one

Kasa Budget shows no ads on its own. On the Kasa Budget Plus screen you can choose "Watch an ad for a day of Plus". Only when you tap it does Kasa Budget load Google's ads service (Google AdMob) and ask it for one video ad. Kasa Budget shows no banners and no other ads.

In the EU, the UK and other places where the law requires it, Google first shows a consent form, where you choose whether Google may use your information for personalised ads. If you don't give the consent Google needs, no ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time under "Ad privacy choices" on the Plus screen.

To show the ad, Google receives information from your phone, such as its advertising ID, IP address, device model and the app it is shown in, and uses it as described in Google's How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Google receives nothing about your transactions, balances or anything else you have entered in Kasa Budget. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google → Ads on most Android phones).

Location

Kasa Budget only uses your location when you tap the location button while adding or editing a transaction. It then reads your current position once and saves it with that transaction. To show a place name instead of coordinates, Kasa Budget asks your phone's built-in location service to look up the address. On Android this service is provided by your phone's maker or Google, and it may send the position online to do so. Kasa Budget does not track your location in the background.

If you tap a saved place to open it on a map, its position is passed to the maps app you choose.

Permissions

Kasa Budget asks for each permission only when you first use the feature that needs it, never when the app starts.

  • Camera: only to take a receipt photo when you choose to. If you pick a photo from your gallery instead, you do it in your phone's own photo picker, and Kasa Budget only receives the photo you pick.
  • Location: only to add a place to a transaction, as described above.
  • Notifications and alarms: only if you turn on the reminders or alerts described below.
  • Google Play billing: to buy Kasa Budget Plus and check whether your Google account owns it.
  • Advertising ID: added by Google's ads service. It is only used when you choose to watch an ad, as described above.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock: only if you turn it on, to unlock Kasa Budget instead of typing your passcode. Your fingerprint or face is checked by your phone. Kasa Budget never receives it, only whether the check succeeded.

If you turn on "shake to hide amounts", Kasa Budget reads the phone's motion sensor while the app is open to notice a shake. These readings are not stored.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Kasa Budget can send you:

  • A daily reminder to add your spending, at the time you choose.
  • A reminder on the morning a scheduled payment is due. It shows the payment's note or category and its amount.
  • A reminder the day before, and on the day, money you marked as a deposit should come back. It shows the entry's note or category and the amount.
  • An alert when a budget reaches 75% or 90%, or is exceeded. It shows the budget's name and the amount left or over.

These notifications are created on your phone by the app itself. No information is sent to the developer or to any notification service to deliver them. Because they can show amounts, they may be visible on your lock screen, depending on your phone's settings. You can turn each kind off in the app, or turn off notifications for Kasa Budget in your device settings.

Import, export and backups

You can import transactions from a file, such as a Spendee export or a CSV or Excel file, and export your transactions to CSV or Excel. You can also save a backup file of all your data and restore it later. Files are read and created on your phone. Kasa Budget does not upload them anywhere.

When you export or back up, your phone's share menu lets you choose where the file goes, for example a folder, a cloud drive or an email. That file then leaves Kasa Budget's control and is handled by the service you chose. Backup and export files are not encrypted and contain your financial records, so keep them somewhere safe.

If you turn on automatic backups, you pick a folder with your phone's own folder picker, and Kasa Budget saves backup files only into that folder. If that folder is synced by a cloud service, the files are handled by that service.

Data sharing

The information you enter in Kasa Budget is not shared with anyone. The developer does not collect it, does not sell, rent or trade it, and does not track you across other apps or websites. Kasa Budget contains no analytics tools. The only third parties involved are Google Play, when you buy or restore Plus, and Google's ads service, only when you choose to watch an ad, as described above.

Security

Kasa Budget keeps your data in its private storage, which other apps cannot read. You can also lock Kasa Budget with a passcode or your fingerprint, and it locks itself again shortly after you leave it. Because your data is stored on your phone, keeping your device secure, for example with a screen lock, also protects your finances.

Your choices and rights

Because your data is stored on your device, you are in control of it: you can view, change and delete your transactions and other records in the app at any time, delete all your data from the app's settings, or delete everything by uninstalling it. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights under data protection law, such as the right to ask what information is held about you or to complain to a data protection authority. The developer holds no information about you, but you are welcome to ask a question or make a request at the email address above.

Not financial advice

Kasa Budget is a tool for keeping track of your own money. Figures such as exchange rates, projections and suggested daily amounts are estimates and can be wrong. They are not financial advice.

Children

Kasa Budget is not intended for children under 16.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Kasa Budget changes, for example when new features are added. Any changes will be published on this page with a new effective date.
