Bills

Regular payments
add themselves.

Rent, phone, salary: set them up once, weekly, monthly, yearly or anything in between, and Kasa adds them on the day. It can remind you the morning a bill is due, and you can pause one or skip a single payment.

Moving in

Bring your
history with you.

Coming from Spendee? Export your data there and open the file in Kasa: your wallets, categories and labels come across with every transaction. Any other app or bank statement that saves a CSV or Excel file works too: match the columns once and Kasa does the rest.

Import a newer Spendee export later and only the new transactions are added. Nothing is doubled.

Extras

Small things that help.

Wallets and currencies

Cash, card, savings: as many wallets as you like, each in its own currency. Over 160 currencies, with exchange rates updated daily.

Find anything

Search by note, amount, category, label or date. Select several transactions to change or delete them at once.

Locked and private

Lock Kasa with a passcode or your fingerprint. Hide every amount with one tap, or by shaking the phone, when someone is looking.

Night owls welcome

Tell Kasa when your day starts, and a taxi home at 2am still counts for the night out it belongs to.

Backup and export

Save everything to one file and restore it on a new phone, or export to CSV or Excel for any dates you choose.

Light and dark

One calm design in both, following your phone's setting or the one you pick.

Privacy

Your money stays
on your phone.

Stored on your device

Transactions, budgets and settings are saved in the app on your phone, not on a server.

No account, no bank login

No sign-up and no password to remember, and Kasa never asks for your bank details. The only thing it downloads is exchange rates.

No ads, no tracking

The app has no ads and no analytics, and your data is never sold.
