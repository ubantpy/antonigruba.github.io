Personal finance · Android

Know where your
money goes.

Kasa is a free, simple app for tracking what you spend and earn. Add an expense in a few taps, set budgets, plan your bills and see each month at a glance. No account and no bank connection: your money stays on your phone.

In development · Android first
Add

An expense in
a few seconds.

Type the amount on the built-in keypad, which can also add, subtract, multiply and divide, so 25 + 5 is just 30. Pick a category and save. Kasa learns from your notes: write "Coffee" again and the category is already picked.

You can also add labels, a receipt photo, the place, another date, a different currency or a repeat. You never have to.

Overview

See where it went,
month by month.

Overview shows your spending by category, money in and out, your balance over time and a few plain numbers: your daily average, how this period compares with the last one, your savings rate and your biggest expense. Tap a category to see what's in it.

Pick any wallets and any period: a week, a month, a year, all time or your own dates.

Budgets

Know how much
is left.

Set a budget for a week, a month, a year or a one-off, for any categories, wallets and labels. Kasa shows how much is left, how much you can spend each day to stay on track, and where you'll end up at your current pace. It lets you know at 75%, at 90% and if you go over.

Bills

Regular payments
add themselves.

Rent, phone, salary: set them up once, from daily to yearly, and Kasa adds them on the day. It can remind you the morning a bill is due, and you can pause one or skip a single payment. You also see your fixed costs and regular income per month.

Moving in

Bring your
history with you.

Coming from Spendee? Export each wallet there as a CSV file and pick them all at once in Kasa. Wallets are matched by name or created, and your categories, labels and notes come across with every transaction. Import again later and only the new transactions are added.

Any other app or bank that saves a CSV or Excel file works too: match the columns once and Kasa does the rest.

Extras

Small things that help.

Wallets and currencies

Cash, card, savings: as many wallets as you like, each in its own currency. Over 160 currencies, with exchange rates updated daily.

Find anything

Search by note, category or amount, and filter by type, wallet, label or date. Long-press to change or delete several transactions at once.

Locked and private

Lock Kasa with a passcode or your fingerprint. Hide every amount with one tap, or by shaking the phone, when someone is looking.

Night owls welcome

Tell Kasa when your day starts, and a taxi home at 2am still counts for the night out it belongs to.

Backup and export

Save everything to one file and restore it on a new phone, or export to CSV or Excel for any dates you choose.

Light and dark

The same calm design in both, following your phone's setting or the one you pick.

Privacy

Your money stays
on your phone.

Stored on your device

Transactions, budgets and settings are saved in the app on your phone, not on a server.

No account, no bank login

No sign-up and no password to remember, and Kasa never asks for your bank details. The only thing it downloads is exchange rates.

No ads, no tracking

The app has no ads and no analytics, and your data is never sold.
